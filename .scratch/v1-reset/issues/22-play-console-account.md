## Question

Create FLO's **personal** Google Play Console developer account. The type was decided in [21](21-closed-test-recruitment-and-run.md). HITL: the user does this, later, at a time they choose.

Steps:
- **Sign-up:** the US$25 fee, identity verification and phone verification. Choose a developer display name; it doesn't have to be your own name.
- **Payments profile and merchant setup,** needed to sell Pro ([08](08-monetisation-timing.md), [19](19-play-billing-research.md)).
- **Check what Play shows publicly** (address and contact details) for a personal account that sells in-app purchases. The user wants to stay anonymous.
- **Create the app entry** with package `app.floexpense.tracker` ([14](14-package-id-firebase.md)) when the first build is ready.

Resolved when the account is verified. The answer records the account type, developer name, payments-profile status and the public-address finding.

This must be done before the first upload of the [safe closed-test build](01-safe-closed-test-build.md).
